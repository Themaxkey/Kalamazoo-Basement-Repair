## Water Out, or Wall Held Up

Almost everything on this list falls into one of two jobs, and confusing them is the single most expensive mistake available to a homeowner.

Waterproofing manages water. [Interior waterproofing](/interior-basement-waterproofing/) intercepts it at the footing and pumps it away; [exterior waterproofing](/exterior-basement-waterproofing/) excavates and stops it reaching the wall at all. Both are drainage work, and neither adds a pound of structural capacity.

Structural repair holds a wall up. [Basement wall repair](/basement-wall-repair/) — carbon fiber, steel bracing, or in severe cases rebuilding — resists soil pressure. It does not make anything dry.

A wall that is both wet and moving needs both, in that order, and any quote that solves one while ignoring the other is only half a job.
