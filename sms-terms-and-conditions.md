## Program Description

Kalamazoo Basement Repair sends text messages to homeowners who have requested contact through this website or by telephone. Messages relate to scheduling an inspection, following up on a request you submitted, and answering questions about the work.

## How You Opt In

You consent to receive text messages by submitting the form on this website with a mobile number, by providing a mobile number during a phone call, or by texting us first.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your inquiry. In most cases it is a small number of messages relating to a single request.

## Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's standard charges apply to any message sent or received. We do not charge for the messages themselves.

## How to Stop

Reply **STOP** to any message to opt out. You will receive a single confirmation and no further messages.

## How to Get Help

Reply **HELP** to any message, or email info@kalamazoobasementrepair.com, or call (269) 307-7363.

## Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy

Mobile numbers collected for text messaging are used for the purposes described above and are not sold. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See the privacy policy for how the rest of your information is handled.

## Supported Carriers

Messaging is supported on major US carriers. Carrier support may change without notice.
